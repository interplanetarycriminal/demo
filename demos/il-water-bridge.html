FLOATING WATER BRIDGE

A high-voltage field polarises and aligns water molecules into a self-supporting dielectric thread that spans the gap between two beakers, held up by Maxwell stress against gravity.

W. G. Armstrong, 1893 · rediscovered by Elmar Fuchs, 2007 · lineage: Armstrong's "rope of water" → Fuchs 2007 high-speed imaging → modern electrohydrodynamics
