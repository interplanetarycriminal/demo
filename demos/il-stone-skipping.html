Stone skipping · the magic angle

A spinning stone planes off the water like a hull; Clanet, Hersen & Bocquet (2004) found the energy lost per bounce is smallest at an attack angle of about 20°, and spin gyroscopically holds that attitude.

Barnes Wallis skipped bouncing bombs across reservoirs in 1943; Clanet, Hersen & Bocquet put a stone-thrower in the lab and published the 20° optimum in Nature (2004); Kurt Steiner's 88-skip world record (2013) is what the theory is chasing.
