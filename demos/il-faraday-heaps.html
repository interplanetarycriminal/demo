Faraday heaps · vibrated sand

Shake a sand layer vertically and it will not stay flat: grains land harder where the layer is already thick, convection carries more grains there, and the bed self-organises into regularly spaced breathing heaps.

Michael Faraday, 1831 — the appendix to the paper that gave us his acoustic figures — reported that vibrated powders gather into heaps. Melo, Umbanhowar & Swinney (1994–96) found the period-doubled oscillons at higher Γ; the whole field of vibrated-bed physics follows.
