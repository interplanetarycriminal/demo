CAMPHOR BOAT
Camphor dissolving off the stern lowers surface tension behind the boat; the intact high-σ water ahead out-pulls it — a surface-tension outboard motor with no moving parts.
Lord Rayleigh, Proc. Roy. Soc. 47, 364 (1890)
Lineage: "camphor dances" reported 1686 (Vogel/Römer) → Rayleigh 1890 measures the film & kills it with a trace of oil → today's self-propelled droplets & Marangoni surfers.
