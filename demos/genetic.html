Genetic Algorithm

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

No gradients and no teacher: score a population of random guesses, let the best breed, add a little noise, and repeat. Good solutions accumulate on their own.

    In the wild

    • Hardware design: NASA's ST5 spacecraft (2006) flew an X-band antenna whose shape an evolutionary algorithm designed.
    • Neural architecture & hyperparameter search: regularized evolution found AmoebaNet image classifiers, and DeepMind's Population-Based Training evolves hyperparameters during training.
    • Neuroevolution for control: NEAT, OpenAI's evolution strategies and Uber's deep GA train game and robot policies with no backpropagation.
