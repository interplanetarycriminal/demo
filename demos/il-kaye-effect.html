Kaye Effect

A falling stream of shear-thinning liquid leaps back out of its own heap: a thin, strongly-sheared slip layer forms at the impact dimple and ricochets the incoming jet like a ramp.
A. Kaye, Nature 1963 · mechanism pinned by high-speed video (Versluis et al., 2000s)
LINEAGE: Kaye's 1963 letter → high-speed-camera slip-layer mechanism → wormlike-micelle fluids generally
