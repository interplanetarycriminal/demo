Honesty: one fixed-size pool, one timeout, no queueing discipline or load shedding. Real cascades also involve GC pauses, connection pools and retries stacking on top.
Lineage. Michael Nygard's Release It! (2007) named the circuit breaker and borrowed bulkheads from ship compartments — seal one flooded section, the hull still floats. Netflix's Hystrix (2012) put breaker + bulkhead + fail-fast in every JVM; Resilience4j, Envoy and Istio carry it now.
