Ant Colony

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

No ant knows the route. Ants that happen to walk short tours leave stronger trails, trails bias the next ants, and evaporation erases the rest, until the colony agrees on a short loop.

    In the wild

    • Network routing. AntNet-style algorithms send small “ant” packets that reinforce fast paths in routing tables; evaporation lets routes adapt when links congest or fail.
    • Logistics and scheduling. ACO variants, usually paired with 2-opt local search, are used for vehicle routing, job-shop scheduling and timetabling where exact solvers are too slow.
    • The ML pattern. Sample solutions from a distribution, reinforce the good ones, decay the rest: the same loop behind the cross-entropy method and policy-gradient RL.

    Another way to see it

    τ is a learned policy over edges. Each round samples tours from it and reinforces them in proportion to reward 1/L, then decays everything. That is the same sample → score → reinforce loop as the cross-entropy method and policy-gradient reinforcement learning.
