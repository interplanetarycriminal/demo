SONOLUMINESCENCE

A standing ultrasound wave traps one bubble that swells slowly, then collapses supersonically — adiabatic compression heats its gas past 10⁴ K and it emits a picosecond blue-white flash, once per acoustic cycle.
H. Frenzel & H. Schultes, 1934; single trapped bubble, D. F. Gaitan, 1989.
Lineage: 1934 multibubble sonoluminescence → Gaitan 1989 single-bubble trap → "a star in a jar".
