LEIDENFROST EFFECT

Far above boiling, a drop floats on its own vapour cushion — the insulating film throttles heat in so hard that the drop outlives one on a merely-hot plate, and skates almost frictionlessly.

Johann Gottlob Leidenfrost, 1756 — De Aquae Communis Nonnullis Qualitatibus Tractatus.
