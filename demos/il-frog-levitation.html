The Levitating Frog

Water is diamagnetic (χ=−9.0e−6), so in a 16 T Bitter solenoid the force χρ/μ₀·B·dB/dz on every molecule balances ρg — and a living frog floats.

Andre Geim & colleagues, 1997 · Ig Nobel 2000 · Nobel 2010 (graphene) — the only person with both
