Evolving Walkers

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

Nobody tells these bodies how to walk: the ones that happen to travel furthest get copied with small random changes, and after a few dozen generations coordinated gaits appear on their own.

    In the wild

    • Robot gaits — evolutionary search tunes legged-robot controllers in simulation before they touch hardware (and the sim-to-real gap is exactly step 7's overfitting).
    • Evolution strategies for RL — OpenAI's ES trained MuJoCo walkers by mutating policy weights and keeping what scored higher, no backprop needed.
    • Neuroevolution & PBT — NEAT evolves network structure with speciation; DeepMind's population-based training breeds hyperparameters for large models.
