Optimizer Race

Every optimizer reads the same gradient — what differs is how it turns that arrow into a step.

    In the wild

    • Adam / AdamW trains virtually every transformer and LLM, paired with a learning-rate warm-up that does a similar job to bias correction.
    • SGD + momentum is still the workhorse for many vision CNNs (ResNets), where it often generalizes a little better than Adam.
    • RMSProp trained DeepMind's Atari DQN agents; memory-lean successors like Adafactor and Lion keep the same per-parameter scaling idea.
