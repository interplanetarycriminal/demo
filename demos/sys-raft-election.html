Lineage. Paxos (Lamport 1989/1998) was correct and famously opaque. Ongaro & Ousterhout, "In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm" (2014) rebuilt consensus around a strong leader, terms as logical time, and randomized election timeouts — designed to be teachable. It now runs etcd (so Kubernetes), Consul, TiKV, CockroachDB, and most modern coordination layers.
