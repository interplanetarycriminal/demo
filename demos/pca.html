PCA · principal axes

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

PCA rotates your view of the data until it lines up with the directions the points actually spread along. Then you can drop the directions that barely vary.

    In the wild

    • Inside LLMs: PCA of hidden activations often shows a few directions that track concepts such as sentiment, truthfulness or refusal. Interpretability and steering-vector work starts from these.
    • Embeddings: 768–4096-D text and image embeddings are projected to 2-D for a first look. Truncating to the top-k PCs also shrinks a vector index and speeds up search.
    • Science & finance: PCA of European genomes reproduces the map of Europe (Novembre 2008). Level, slope and curvature explain most bond-yield-curve moves. Eigenfaces applied the same idea to faces.
