K-Means Clustering

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

K-means finds k groups by alternating two dead-simple moves — join the nearest centre, move each centre to the middle of its points — until nothing changes.

    In the wild

    • Vector search for RAG: FAISS-style IVF indexes run k-means over millions of embeddings, then search only the few nearest cells instead of everything.
    • Discrete "tokens" for audio & images: HuBERT-style speech models and VQ codebooks cluster feature vectors with k-means so a transformer can predict unit IDs like words.
    • Colour quantisation & segmentation: cluster pixel colours into a k-colour palette, or customers into segments; k-means also seeds Gaussian mixture models.
