COANDĂ EFFECT

A jet clings to a curved surface because it entrains the air in the gap; the starved gap's pressure drops and the ambient shoves the jet onto the wall.

Henri Coandă, 1930s — noted a century earlier by Thomas Young (1800).

LINEAGE: Young's 19th-c. note → Coandă names the effect → NOTAR helicopters & fluidic logic devices.
