Honesty: leader election is abstracted to a countdown — real elections are Raft/Paxos voting rounds with randomized timeouts, and a fencing token or STONITH is what actually stops the stale leader.
Lineage. Jim Gray framed the partition problem for distributed transactions (1981); Brewer's CAP conjecture (2000) was proved by Gilbert & Lynch (2002); Raft (2014) made majority rule teachable — a leader needs votes from >N/2, so two leaders cannot both hold a majority. Fencing tokens: the old leader's writes are rejected because its token number is stale; STONITH ("shoot the other node in the head") does it with a power cable.
