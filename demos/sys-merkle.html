Honesty: hashes are shown truncated to 6 hex digits and collisions are ignored — a real tree uses SHA-256, and a real repair also has to decide which side is newer.
Lineage. Ralph Merkle patented the hash tree in 1979 as a way to sign many messages with one root. Dynamo (2007) used it for anti-entropy between replicas — Cassandra and Riak still do. git commits, IPFS and every blockchain are the same idea: one root hash stands for a whole world of data, and equal roots mean you can stop looking.
