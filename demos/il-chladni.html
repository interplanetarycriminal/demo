Ernst Chladni (1787) drew a violin bow down a sand-strewn brass plate and made sound visible. Napoleon set a prize for the mathematics; Sophie Germain won it in 1816 with the fourth-order plate equation. Lineage: Chladni's bow → Napoleon's prize → Germain 1816 → Kirchhoff → modern cymatics & modal testing. Tap the plate to re-scatter the sand.
