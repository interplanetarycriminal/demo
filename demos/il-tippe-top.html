TIPPE TOP

Sliding friction at the contact point torques the spin axis over while angular momentum stays near-vertical, so the top flips onto its stem and RAISES its own centre of mass.

1950s: Braams, Hugenholtz · the Bohr–Pauli photo · modern account: Moffatt & Shimomura (2002)

LINEAGE: 19th-c. wooden toys → 1950s Bohr–Pauli photo & first mechanics → Moffatt–Shimomura 2002. Reduced model — honest about it.
