K-Nearest Neighbors

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

kNN learns no formula: it keeps every labelled example, and for a new point it lets the k closest ones vote.

    In the wild

    • Retrieval for LLMs (RAG). A question is embedded and the k nearest document chunks, by cosine distance, are pasted into the prompt. Vector databases are kNN engines.
    • Recommenders and search. “People like you also liked…” and reverse image search are nearest-neighbour lookups in a learned embedding space.
    • Anomaly detection. The distance to your k-th nearest neighbour is a classic outlier score for fraud and faulty sensors.

    3 · Score it

    acc = (1/N) Σi [ ŷ(xi) = yi ]
    train · held-out

    Train accuracy asks each of the N = training points to predict its own label, and it sits inside its own neighbourhood. Held-out accuracy uses 320 fresh points labelled by the true shapes.

    Misconception: “k = 1 scores 100% on the training data, so it is the best k.” Every point is its own nearest neighbour, so k = 1 always scores 100% there. That measures memory, not skill: pick k on held-out data.
