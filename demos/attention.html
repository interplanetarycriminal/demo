Self-Attention

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

Every word rewrites itself as a weighted blend of the others: it asks a question (query), every word advertises what it has (key), and the best matches hand over their information (value).

    In the wild

    • Every LLM layer (GPT, Claude, Llama) runs dozens of these heads in parallel; interpretability research has found real previous-token, induction and coreference heads.
    • Causal masking is why models generate left to right; the KV cache stores past keys and values so each new token computes only one new query row.
    • Beyond text: Vision Transformers attend between image patches, AlphaFold between amino-acid residues, text-to-image models between words and pixels.

    √d is not cosmetic. If q and k have d independent unit-variance components, q·k has variance d. At d = 128 raw scores swing by ±11, softmax turns one-hot and gradients vanish. Dividing by √d keeps the variance near 1.
    A weight is not an explanation. aij ≠ aji (WQ ≠ WK), and a big weight says where information was read from, not why the model decided anything.

    Honest caveat: these embeddings and matrices are hand-wired (11 dims, dk = 4) so every number means something, and all three heads share one WV so their value bars stay comparable (real heads each learn their own). A trained model runs exactly this math with learned matrices of size ~4096×128, whose dimensions rarely have clean names.
