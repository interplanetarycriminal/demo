CIRCUMHORIZONTAL ARC

Hexagonal ice plates in cirrus take sun in a vertical side face and out the flat bottom — a 90° prism that paints a spectral band under the sun.
classical halo optics — Pernter & Exner, 1910
LINEAGE: 22° halo / sundogs → circumhorizontal "fire rainbow" (no fire, no rain, no bow) → circumzenithal arc
