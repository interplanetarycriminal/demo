LENZ JUMPING RING

AC flux through an iron-cored coil drives a current in the ring that opposes the change, so the ring is thrown upward — unless its loop is cut.
Lenz 1834; Elihu Thomson's lecture ring, 1887
LINEAGE: Faraday 1831 induction → Lenz 1834 direction → Thomson's jumping ring
