COLLADON'S LIGHT FOUNTAIN

A falling water jet traps a laser beam by total internal reflection, so the light bends with the liquid — until the jet curves too steeply and the beam leaks out.

Jean-Daniel Colladon, 1842, Geneva

LINEAGE: Colladon 1842 → Tyndall's 1854 Royal Institution encore → optical fibre

Drag JET SPEED: a fast, flat jet guides light far; a slow jet curves down fast and light sprays out early.
