Cartesian Diver

Squeezing the bottle compresses the diver's trapped air (Boyle), raising its mean density past the water's — buoyancy loses and it sinks.
Named for Descartes · Boyle 1662 (P·V=const) · Archimedes (buoyancy)
LINEAGE: 17th-c. parlour divers → Boyle 1662 → Archimedes' principle
