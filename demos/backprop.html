Backpropagation

Space play · ←/→ tour · R reset

Backprop is the chain rule run backwards: each weight’s gradient is the signal that went through it times the blame that came back to it.

    In the wild

    • loss.backward() in PyTorch and grad in JAX run exactly this reverse sweep (reverse-mode autodiff) over any computation graph.
    • Training an LLM is this loop over billions of weights. Forward activations are cached for the backward pass, which is why training needs far more memory than inference.
    • Vanishing gradients (tour step 7) drove the move to ReLU, residual connections, normalisation layers and cross-entropy losses.
