REVERSE SPRINKLER

Run a sprinkler backwards — sucking — and the inflowing momentum flux is almost exactly cancelled by pressure forces on the inside of the bends; only vortex losses survive, so it creeps slowly BACKWARD.

Ernst Mach, 1883 · Feynman, Princeton 1940s · settled ~2000s
