TIN PEST — METAL THAT ROTS

Below 13.2 °C white β-tin is only metastable: grey α-tin nucleates at a scratch and spreads autocatalytically, and being 27% less dense it bursts the metal into powder.

Cold speeds the rot until about −30 °C, then diffusion dies and it slows again — the nose of a TTT curve. Warming back up stops it but never reverses it.

Lineage · Aristotle-era reports of "tin plague" → the 1868 St Petersburg organ pipes crumbling in the cold → today's fear of tin whiskers and pest in lead-free solder.
