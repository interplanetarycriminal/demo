TEARS OF WINE
Alcohol evaporates fastest from the ultrathin meniscus film, so surface tension σ rises there; the high-σ ring pulls liquid up the glass until the ridge overloads and beads streak back down as tears.
James Thomson, 1855 — a decade before Marangoni
Lineage: Thomson 1855 (evaporation-driven flow) → Marangoni 1865 (the name) → Gibbs (thermodynamic footing) → today: coffee-ring & thin-film Marangoni theory.
ABV 13% air dryness (evaporation) dry 0% ABV = dead calm. Strength is non-monotonic: it peaks near ~20% then falls — the film runs out of σ contrast.
