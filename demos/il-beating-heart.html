MERCURY BEATING HEART

SIMULATION ONLY — NO APPARATUS
SAFETY / HISTORY: the original used liquid mercury and strong oxidisers. Mercury vapour is a potent cumulative neurotoxin, so this bench classic was retired from teaching. This page is a numerical model of the published dynamics — no procedure, reagents, or quantities are given, and none should be attempted.

An oxide skin forms on the metal drop and raises surface tension until the drop pulls in, touches the iron needle, the contact strips the film, tension drops, the blob spreads off the needle — a relaxation oscillator that beats.

Gerboin, early 1800s · lineage: Gerboin's pulsating globule → a century of lecture benches (Lippmann's electrocapillarity) → retired on mercury toxicity → survives in the Belousov–Zhabotinsky family of chemical oscillators
