GREEN FLASH

The atmosphere is a weak prism: it refracts blue light more than red, so the sun's red disc sets first and for a heartbeat only the green rim still clears the horizon.
Popularized by Jules Verne, "Le Rayon Vert" 1882; explained as atmospheric dispersion + mirage magnification
