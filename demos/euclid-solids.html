BOOK XIII inscribes each solid in a sphere and compares its edge to the diameter: XIII.13 pyramid, .14 octahedron, .15 cube, .16 icosahedron, .17 dodecahedron. XIII.18 lays the five edges side by side — pyramid > octahedron > cube > icosahedron > dodecahedron — then closes the Elements. Plato's Timaeus gave four to the elements and kept the dodecahedron for the cosmos.
